Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chewsy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the Chewsy mobile application ("the App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Your name, username, and email address when you create an account.
  • Profile information: Your profile photo (if provided via Google sign-in), avatar colour preference, and dietary preferences.
  • Location data: Your approximate location is used to find nearby restaurants. We do not store your location permanently.
  • Usage data: Information about how you use the App, including sessions created, restaurants swiped on, and matches made. This is used to power your Taste Profile and stats.
  • Device information: Your device's push notification token, to send you session invites and match notifications.
  • Referral data: If you refer a friend or use a referral code, we record the referral relationship.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the App and its features
  • Find nearby restaurants based on your location
  • Connect you with friends and enable group sessions
  • Send you push notifications for session invites, friend requests, and matches
  • Display your Taste Profile and usage statistics
  • Process premium subscriptions and referral rewards
  • Improve the App and fix bugs
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Your display name, username, and profile photo are visible to other users you connect with in the App.
  • With service providers: We use third-party services to operate the App, including Supabase (database), Google Places API (restaurant data), Google OAuth (sign-in), Expo (push notifications), and AdMob (advertising for free users). These providers have their own privacy policies.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal information for as long as you have an account with us. If you delete your account, your personal data is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data (via Profile → Delete Account in the App)
  • Opt out of push notifications (via your device settings)
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise these rights, contact us at smryan1986@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, use, or disclosure. All data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security policies. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Places API, OAuth, AdMob)
  • Supabase
  • Expo

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
